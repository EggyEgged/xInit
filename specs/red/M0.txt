4. Re-run 2026-11-23: signal safety (defect 1) and B-M0-023 flake (defect 2)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Defect 1. Every signal either suite sends now goes through one of:
- xrginit itself: pidfd opened right after spawn, before the waiter thread can reap it.
- a reported service pid: only if verify_child() saw it as a direct child of that xrginit
  (stat field 4) with pid > 1; its start time (field 22) is recorded then and re-checked
  after pidfd_open, and the signal goes through that pidfd.
- the service's process group: same checks, plus field 5 == pid (it leads its group);
  PIDFD_SIGNAL_PROCESS_GROUP, falling back to kill(-pgid) only on EINVAL with pgid > 1.
  That fallback is the only raw kill() left in either suite.
- the B-M0-019 grandchild (pid from the service's pidfile): only if its environ carries a
  unique per-test guard entry and its start time is unchanged, then via pidfd.
If the check fails, nothing is signalled. A test that needs the signal panics with
"refusing to signal the service: pid Some(<n>) ...". Cleanup (Run::drop, Proc::drop,
KillOnDrop) skips the signal silently.
Sites: tests/common/mod.rs (Run::drop, signal_xrginit, signal_service[_group]),
cli_lifecycle.rs (b_m0_015, KillOnDrop/b_m0_019), service_env.rs (b_m0_020 group kill),
holdout blackbox.rs (Proc::drop, Proc::signal, holdout_b_m0_015, _019, _020_022).

Defect 2. b_m0_023_only_fds_0_1_2_open (and the B-M0-023 part of the holdout's
holdout_b_m0_021_023_024_027_process_state, now from a second run) read /proc/<P>/fd only
after a FIFO readiness line. The service is
  sh -c "trap 'kill $!; exit 0' TERM; (echo ready > \"$1\"; exec sleep ..) & wait"
The readiness write happens in a forked subshell, so the service's own fd table is never
touched, and the service has finished loading before it forks. No other test reads
/proc/<P>/fd. The other /proc reads after the running line (cmdline, exe, signal masks,
pgrp/sid, ids, cwd, environ) are all fixed at exec, so they cannot race the loader.
